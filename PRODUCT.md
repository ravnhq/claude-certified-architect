# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Ravn people across all roles — not only engineers — preparing for the Anthropic
partner certifications: Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (CCAR-F),
Architect – Professional (CCAR-P), and Developer – Foundations (CCDV-F). Reading
level and platform experience therefore vary widely inside the same audience; the
material cannot assume an engineering background it does not first supply.

The site is published openly at
<https://ravnhq.github.io/claude-certified-architect/>, so partner-network
practitioners outside Ravn read it too. They are welcome, not the design target.

## Product Purpose

Take a Ravn candidate from "I am thinking about booking" to a passing score.
The site carries the whole arc: study guides per track, practice exams on the real
blueprint weights and the real 720/1000 cut, a browsable question bank, a
cheatsheet, a preflight readiness checklist, and a score-report reader that turns
a failed or marginal attempt into a targeted study plan.

Success has two parts, both confirmed: candidates sit the exam prepared and pass,
and the body of work demonstrates Ravn's depth on the Claude platform.

## Positioning

Blueprint fidelity, and the fact that it is built by people who sat the exams.
Every practice item is tagged to an official task statement or objective, exams
draw at the published domain weights, scoring reproduces the real scale, and
validators fail the build on a bad answer key, an off-blueprint objective, or a
domain bank too small for its draw. Foundations material is authored in English,
Spanish, and Portuguese. No competing prep source combines official-blueprint
tagging, three tracks, tri-language Foundations, and a score-report-driven
personalized plan.

## Operating Context

Two study scenes, both confirmed and both first-class:

- **Desktop, long sessions.** Reading a full guide, running a timed 53/60/63-item
  exam, reviewing a score report. Wide viewport, sustained attention.
- **Phone, short drills.** Working the question bank or a single domain in spare
  moments. Small viewport, interrupted attention, progress must survive a tab
  closing.

Print and last-minute cramming are not the scenes this was built for. PDFs exist
and regenerate on deploy, but design decisions are not made for paper.

Everything runs in the browser with no account and no backend. Progress, theme,
preflight ticks, and personalized study data live in `localStorage` on one device
and do not sync.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site under `docs/`, deployed to GitHub Pages. No framework: hand-written
  HTML/CSS/JS, generated by Python builders in `utils/` and `scripts/build-pages.mjs`.
  Vendored runtime dependencies only (`marked`, `minisearch`, `pdfjs-dist`).
- Three tracks with different blueprints: Foundations five domains, Developer
  eight, Professional seven. Material from one track never stands in for another.
- Foundations is tri-language (EN/ES/PT). Professional and Developer are English
  only, because Anthropic delivers those exams in English and forbids browser
  translation during proctoring; ES/PT would train against wording the exam never
  uses.
- The Foundations bank is Ravn-authored and hand-edited. The CCAR-P and CCDV-F
  banks are generated from the practice-bank corpus by `utils/import_practice_bank.py`;
  direct edits to them are lost on the next import.
- Exam and cheatsheet HTML is generated. Edit sources, never `dist/`, and
  regenerate every exam, bank, and cheatsheet after a style change.
- Full-text search across all languages, a language switcher, exam and study
  modes, per-domain breakdown, domain drill, and PDF export.
- Score-report upload is parsed client-side; the PDF and candidate details are
  never stored or transmitted.

## Brand Commitments

Ravn identity, extracted from the live ravn.com and recorded in `DESIGN.md`:
dark neutral surfaces, Inter headings and prose, JetBrains Mono for code and
identifiers, white primary actions, pale gold used sparingly. The Ravn wordmark
is preserved. Voice is plain, factual, and specific — the README states figures,
prices, and policy dates outright and marks what is subject to change.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official exam guides for all three tracks under `ccaf/sources/`, `ccap/sources/`,
  `ccdf/sources/`; the certification FAQ for delivery and policy facts.
- Real question banks: 607 English Foundations items, plus the imported CCAR-P and
  CCDV-F banks under `.corpus/practice-bank/`.
- Firecrawl captures of ravn.com in `.firecrawl/`, with scrape IDs, behind `DESIGN.md`.

Absences future work must not paper over: there are no pass-rate statistics, no
testimonials, no case studies, and no endorsement from Anthropic. The material is
not real exam content and says so; that disclaimer stays. Partner Academy course
content is gated and must never be committed or quoted.

## Product Principles

1. **Blueprint fidelity over volume.** An item tagged to a real objective at the
   real weight beats ten untagged ones. Validators enforce this; design must not
   hide the tagging that earns the claim.
2. **Honest about what it is.** Practice, not prediction. State figures, sources,
   and what Anthropic marks subject to change. Never imply official status.
3. **Two scenes, one product.** A long desktop reading session and a two-minute
   phone drill are both the real thing. Neither is a degraded version of the other.
4. **The candidate's data is theirs.** No account, no backend, no upload. Local
   storage, clearable, and said plainly.
5. **Three tracks stay distinct.** Never blur Foundations, Developer, and
   Professional material, weights, or language coverage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Content must be usable by non-engineer Ravn colleagues without platform jargon
going unexplained. Visible keyboard focus, honored reduced-motion preferences,
and content visible without scroll reveals are existing commitments in `DESIGN.md`.
No formal conformance level has been established — this is an open decision, not
a settled one.
