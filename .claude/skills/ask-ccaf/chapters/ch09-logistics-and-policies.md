# Chapter 9: Registration, Policies, Exam Day, and Recertification

*Covers guide sections 11–16. This chapter is administrative — thin on technical content by nature, but the retake waiting periods and the 12-month validity window are the facts most candidates get wrong.*

## Core Idea
Registration runs through **Anthropic Partner Academy plus Pearson VUE**, and the credential lasts **12 months** with a free non-proctored renewal — but only if you renew before it lapses.

## Registration and scheduling (6 steps)
1. Go to the certification page for your exam on the Anthropic Partner Academy and review exam details.
2. Download the Exam Guide and review the Certification Terms and Conditions and the Certification Exam Policy **before registering**.
3. Register and complete checkout — the fee shown reflects any discount for your partner tier.
4. Follow the confirmation instructions to create your Pearson VUE account, then sign in to schedule.
5. Choose a date and select **online proctoring or a Pearson test center**; delivery is by Pearson VUE.
6. Cancel or reschedule **up to 24 hours before** your appointment; changes inside 24 hours forfeit the fee.

## Reference Tables

### Deadlines and limits
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Reschedule/cancel window | up to 24 hours before the appointment; inside 24 hours forfeits the fee |
| No-show or late arrival | forfeits the fee, must re-register |
| Retake wait — after 1st fail | **14 days** |
| Retake wait — after 2nd fail | **30 days** |
| Retake wait — after 3rd fail | **90 days** |
| Attempts allowed | up to **4 per exam** in a rolling 12-month period; the fee applies to each attempt |
| Credential validity | 12 months from award date |
| Appeal window | **14 days** from notification, or from the exam date for a result concern |

Limits apply **per exam** — failing one does not block registering for a different one.

### Identification and accommodations
| Topic | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ID | valid, unexpired, **government-issued photo ID**; name must match registration **exactly** |
| Name correction | contact certifications-support@anthropic.com **before scheduling** |
| Accommodations | requested and **approved by Pearson VUE before you schedule**; do not schedule until approved; request at pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations |

### Exam-day rules of conduct
During the exam you must:
- Remain within view of the proctor and webcam for the entire session, if testing online.
- Keep your workspace clear of notes, books, phones, secondary monitors, and other materials.
- Refrain from communicating with any other person.
- Not capture, copy, photograph, or reproduce any exam content in any form.

Prohibited items: mobile phones, smart watches, headphones, study materials, any recording device. Permitted items, if any — such as scratch paper provided by the proctor — are specified by Pearson VUE.

**Consequences of misconduct:** cheating, attempting to access prohibited resources, or disclosing exam content may result in invalidation of your result, revocation of your credential, and a ban from future exams.

## Confidentiality and NDA
Before the exam begins you must accept a confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement: all exam content — questions, answer options, scenarios — is the confidential and proprietary property of Anthropic, and you agree not to disclose, reproduce, or distribute any portion of it. **Declining ends the session with no refund.**

## Credential maintenance and recertification
- Valid **12 months** from award; time-limited because the underlying technology evolves rapidly.
- **On-time renewal:** review what has changed since you certified and complete a **free, non-proctored assessment** on the Anthropic Partner Academy. No fee.
- **Lapsed credential:** retake the full exam at full fee to regain certified status.
- If exam content changes significantly, Anthropic may require holders to **retake the full exam** rather than complete the renewal assessment.
- Holders remain subject to the rules of conduct in Section 13.

## Support, appeals, privacy
| Need | Contact |
|---|---|
| Name correction on registration | certifications-support@anthropic.com |
| Registration, scheduling, accommodations, results | Pearson VUE — pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html |
| Appeals and complaints | Pearson VUE support, within 14 days |

**Not subject to appeal:** the standard-setting outcome and the content of individual exam items.

Personal data collected during registration and testing is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy, Pearson VUE's privacy policy, and applicable data-protection law.

## Anti-patterns
- **Scheduling before accommodations are approved** — approval must come first.
- **Registering with a name that does not exactly match your photo ID** — fix it before scheduling.
- **Rescheduling inside 24 hours** — the fee is forfeited.
- **Letting the credential lapse** — renewal is free and non-proctored; a lapse costs the full exam fee.
- **Assuming a failed attempt can be retaken immediately** — 14, then 30, then 90 days.
- **Appealing item content or the cut score** — neither is appealable.

## Key Takeaways
1. Two systems: Anthropic Partner Academy to register, Pearson VUE to schedule and deliver.
2. 24 hours is the reschedule line; missing the appointment forfeits the fee.
3. Retake waits escalate 14 → 30 → 90 days, capped at 4 attempts per rolling 12 months.
4. Renew on time and it is free and non-proctored; lapse and you retake the whole exam.
5. Accepting the NDA is mandatory to start, and disclosure risks revocation and a ban.
6. Appeals go to Pearson VUE within 14 days, and cannot touch item content or the cut score.
